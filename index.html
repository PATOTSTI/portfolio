HELLO AGAIN. I'M VINCENT.

IT STARTED
WITH IRON MAN.

It grew through building.

As a kid, I loved computers. Watching Iron Man made the idea of building technology feel exciting. That curiosity led me to Computer Engineering.

I first connected with the hardware side. I built my own PC and learned to troubleshoot it myself. At university, I discovered how much the field also depends on software. Learning to code opened up a different way to solve problems.

Since then, I've worked on Python automation during my internship and helped develop Hydrobot & Aquadetect with my thesis team. I'm still drawn to the same thing: understanding a problem, learning what it takes, and making something work.
