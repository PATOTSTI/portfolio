Hydrobot is the physical platform in our team's oil-spill detection and filtration thesis.

We designed and built an autonomous RC boat with an ESP32, GPS, and IMU, bringing embedded hardware and navigation into the project. A natural-fiber filtration system explored material ratios for oil absorption.

AquaDetect is the connected software side: a dashboard for telemetry, mapping, and detection results. Together, the two parts connect what happens in the physical system with what an operator can see on screen.

CONNECTING
THE HARDWARE.

The ESP32 collects sensor data, processes control signals, and transmits information wirelessly. The circuit connects those decisions to the boat's motors and water pump.

01 / SENSE & LOCATE

Know the conditions.

A capacitive sensor, NEO-M8N GPS, and ICM-20948 compass module provide surface-condition, position, and orientation data.

02 / DISTRIBUTE POWER

Supply each subsystem.

A 12V Li-ion battery with BMS and a 12V solar panel form the power system, alongside step-down converters, fuses, and rocker switches.

03 / MOVE & FILTER

Turn signals into action.

Two BTS7960 drivers control the brushed DC motors. A relay switches the 12V water pump to move collected water through filtration.

FLOTATION & BALANCE

Twin hulls

Two sealed PVC hulls provide flotation and balance for the electronics, filtration chamber, and pump. Lightweight, water-resistant materials and sealed joints help limit water ingress.

110 × 10 × 18 cm each

STRUCTURE & PROTECTION

Electronics enclosure

A Sintra-board body reinforced with fiberglass and epoxy resin houses the controller, battery, sensors, and wiring. It adds structural support and protection from moisture.

42 × 26 × 32 cm

OIL ABSORPTION & WATER FLOW

Filtration chamber

An engineering-plastic chamber holds human and dog hair filtration media. A pump-connected hose feeds the chamber; side outlets let filtered water exit, while a gasket helps limit leakage.

18 × 17 × 10 cm

PANEL SUPPORT & POSITIONING

Solar mount

A lightweight engineering-plastic frame supports the solar panel above the body. Its placement improves exposure to sunlight while keeping the panel clear of other components.

17 × 22 × 20 cm

SURFACE-WATER INTAKE

Oil collection mouth

The stainless-steel intake collects oil-contaminated surface water and provides mounts for the pump connection and capacitive sensor. Openings near the waterline guide water toward filtration and help screen out larger debris.

17 × 16 × 20 cm
