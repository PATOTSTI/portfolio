EPHOR helps university HR teams and supervisors turn competency assessments into targeted learning & development plans.

Built for web and Android, the system automates Competency Assessment Training Needs Analysis (CATNA) processing. A FastAPI service with Google Gemini analyzes assessment data, recommends training, groups personnel with similar needs, and flags retraining needs after impact assessments.

Role-based dashboards and an in-app AI assistant, Augustus, support the workflow from assessment to follow-up.
